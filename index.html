Our approach

Useful on its own. Better when connected.

The name Onjaal comes from the idea of a net: separate tools that become more valuable when they connect.

We want every Onjaal product to be useful by itself and, over time, to share a design language and a foundation with the others. We are at the start: Onjaal HRMS comes first.

Onjaal HRMSIn development
Shared design and foundation
Future productsNone announced

  1. Understandable without training

    Someone should be able to work out what to do from the screen in front of them.

  2. Everyday work deserves good design

    Finding a record or following a request should be calm and clear, not an afterthought.

  3. Connection has to be practical

    Products should connect when it saves real effort, not to make a longer list.

  4. Claims match the product

    We describe what exists, label what is in progress, and leave out what we can’t yet stand behind.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does HRMS mean?

It stands for human resource management system. It is software that keeps employee records and everyday HR tasks, such as leave requests, in one place.

Can I use Onjaal HRMS today?

Not yet. Onjaal HRMS is in development. The interface on this page is a design concept with fictional data, not a working product.

Which features will it have?

We haven’t published a confirmed feature list, and we won’t list features until they are real. Pricing and release timing are not announced either.

Does Onjaal have other products?

No. Onjaal HRMS is the first and only product we are working on publicly. Connecting future products is a long-term direction.
