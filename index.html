Onjaal HRMS · In development

Better‑organized HR. Less everyday friction.

Onjaal HRMS is being built to keep employee information and everyday HR workflows in one clear, organized place, for HR teams, employees and administrators.

Explore HRMS Talk to us

Onjaal HRMS is the first product from Onjaal, a business technology company building practical software for growing businesses. The interface shown is an illustrative concept.

What Onjaal HRMS is for

Everyday HR work shouldn’t depend on knowing where to look.

01

Employee information, in one place

Records spread across spreadsheets, folders and inboxes are slow to find and easy to let go out of date. HRMS is designed to give them one organized home.

02

Workflows people can follow

Routine tasks, such as a leave request, should have a clear path from the first step to the last, so nobody has to guess what happens next.

03

Request status you can see

Employees shouldn’t have to ask where a request stands, and HR shouldn’t have to keep answering. Status should be visible to the people involved.

04

A view for each role

HR teams, employees and administrators need different things from the same information. HRMS is designed around those differences.

How it works

One workflow, from record to decision.

The intended flow of Onjaal HRMS, using leave as the example.

  1. Keep people information together

    HR maintains employee records in a single directory, so everyone works from the same details.

  2. Send and follow requests

    Employees submit routine requests and can see at any point where each one stands.

  3. Review and decide

    Reviewers act on requests in one place, and administrators keep visibility across the organization.

Intended workflow, subject to change as the product is built. Onjaal HRMS is not yet available.

The Onjaal vision

Useful on its own. Better connected.

The name Onjaal comes from the idea of a net: separate pieces that become more valuable when they connect.

We want each Onjaal product to stand on its own and, over time, work with the others through a shared design philosophy and a shared foundation.

We are at the start. Onjaal HRMS is our first product and it is still in development. No other product has been announced.
