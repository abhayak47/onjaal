Onjaal HRMS

HR operations, organized in one place.

For HR teams

A clear home for people-related work, so less time goes to chasing information.

For employees

A straightforward experience that makes everyday HR tasks easier to find and complete.

For business administrators

Better visibility across the organization, with a foundation that can grow with the business.

Solutions

Simpler operations start with connected tools.

Growing businesses often end up with disconnected tools and repeated work. Onjaal aims to reduce that friction.

01

Less switching

Bring everyday business tasks into purpose-built software, instead of spreading them across spreadsheets, inboxes and separate apps.

02

Clearer information

When tools share a foundation, information has one home, which makes it easier to find, trust and act on.

03

Room to grow

Start with what you need today, and add connected capabilities as the business changes, without starting over.

Our approach

Principles we build by.

These are the commitments guiding how we design and build Onjaal products.

Usability first

Software should be understandable without a manual. We design for clarity before complexity.

Connected workflows

Each product is planned as part of a whole, so work can flow between tools.

Thoughtful design

Consistent, considered interfaces that respect people's time and attention.

Honest communication

We tell you what is available today and what is still being built.

About Onjaal

Useful technology, woven together.

"Jaal" means a net or web, and that idea sits at the center of Onjaal: a business technology brand building software where the pieces strengthen each other.

Too much business software is powerful but hard to use, or simple but isolated. We want to build tools that are practical on their own and more valuable together.

We're starting with Onjaal HRMS and growing the ecosystem carefully, one well-made product at a time.
